## Notes

- Gemma 3's 4B, 12B, and 27B variants all accept text + image input with text output; the 1B variant is text-only and is listed separately under [Lightweight & Edge AI](lightweight-edge-ai.md).
- Google's Gemma license is a custom permissive license, not Apache/MIT — classify as **Commercial Allowed**, not plain "Open Source," when citing it.
- Llama 4 (see [General AI](general-ai.md)) is also multimodal (text + image in, text out) and can be cross-referenced here for larger-scale vision-language needs.

This category currently has one dedicated entry beyond the cross-references above. Additional Vision-Language, Audio-Language, and Video-Language models are welcome — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).
